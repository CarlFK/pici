Term tips:

  • Demo Button: Linux on KianV RISC-V - sends "mpremote" keystrokes to wssh
  • tmux: ^ad - Disconnect (drops ssh connection)
  • ^c^c - KeyboardInterrupt little_term.py drops to MicroPython REPL
  • MicroPython ^D - Resets TinyTapeout board.
  • mpremote: ^x - exit (drops to bash shell on Pi)
  • tio: ^tq - quit (drops to bash shell on Pi)
